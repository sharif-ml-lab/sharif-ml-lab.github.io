What is MLL Lab?

The Machine Learning Lab (MLL), under the supervision of Dr. Soleymani, is a cutting-edge research center based in Sharif University of Technology, Tehran, Iran. MLL is dedicated to exploring a wide range of critical topics in the field of machine learning, from generalization to compositionality.

Research Area

  • Generalization
  • Compositional Learning
  • Reinforcement Learning
  • Generative Models
  • Vision-language Models

Projects

Compositional Generation

Text-to-Image Models

In recent years, text-to-image (T2I) diffusion models such as Stable Diffusion and DALL-E have shown promising performance in generating realistic, creative, diverse, and high-quality images from textual descriptions. These models leverage the iterative denoising process and text embeddings through the cross-attention mechanism to generate images used in many applications across various domains.

Compositional Misalignments

Despite impressive capabilities, T2I models often struggle to faithfully capture all the entities, attributes, and relationships described in the input prompt, leading to various compositional misalignments such as entity missing, improper attribute binding, wrong spatial relationships, and counting problems.

Research Goal

We aim to add compositional generation capability into T2I models to overcome the mentioned compositional generation failure modes and enable more accurate and faithful image generation from complex textual descriptions.

Spurious Correlation

Problem Definition

Spurious correlations occur when machine learning models learn to rely on superficial patterns or biases in the training data rather than the true underlying relationships. This can lead to poor generalization and biased predictions in real-world scenarios.

Research Focus

Our research focuses on developing methods to identify and mitigate spurious correlations in machine learning models, enabling more robust and reliable predictions across diverse datasets and domains.

Generalization

Out-of-Distribution Generalization

Investigating how machine learning models can maintain performance when encountering data distributions that differ from the training distribution, which is crucial for real-world deployment scenarios.

Domain Adaptation

Developing techniques to transfer knowledge learned from one domain to another, enabling models to adapt and perform well across different but related domains with minimal additional training.

Reinforcement Learning

Model-Based Approaches

Exploring reinforcement learning methods that build and leverage internal models of the environment to plan and make decisions, improving sample efficiency and enabling better long-term planning.

Multi-Agent Systems

Research on systems where multiple agents interact, cooperate, or compete to achieve individual or collective goals, with applications in robotics, game theory, and distributed systems.
